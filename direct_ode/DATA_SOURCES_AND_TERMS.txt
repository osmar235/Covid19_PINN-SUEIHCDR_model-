Frozen inputs and provider terms

The exact snapshots are included so reproduction does not depend on live URLs
or on a provider continuing to distribute the same vintage. All are public
aggregate datasets. Original provider terms continue to govern these files.
The software MIT license does not grant additional rights to third-party data.

USAFacts: county cases, report-date deaths and population.
  covid_confirmed_usafacts.csv
  covid_deaths_usafacts.csv
  covid_county_population_usafacts.csv
  Provider: USAFacts, usafacts.org

US HHS: facility-level weekly admissions and completeness input.
  data_epi/COVID-19_Reported_Patient_Impact_and_Hospital_Capacity_by_Facility.csv
  Historical federal open-data provider: healthdata.gov

California public health: hospital census (secondary comparison), vaccination,
variant shares and testing.
  covid19hospitalbycounty.csv
  data_epi/statewide-covid-19-vaccines-administered-by-county-booster-metric-archived.csv
  data_epi/covid19_variants.csv
  data_epi/statewide-covid-19-cases-deaths-tests.csv
  Providers: California Health and Human Services/CDPH public-data catalogues.

US CDC: commercial and blood-donor serosurveys and lineage proportions.
  data_epi/Nationwide_Commercial_Laboratory_Seroprevalence_Survey.csv
  data_epi/2020-2021_Nationwide_Blood_Donor_Seroprevalence_Survey_Infection-Induced_Seroprevalence_Estimates.csv
  data_epi/2022_2023_Nationwide_Blood_Donor_Seroprevalence_Survey_Combined_Infection-_and_Vaccination-Induced_Seroprevalence_Estimates.csv
  data_epi/jr58-6ysp.csv
  Historical provider: data.cdc.gov (jr58-6ysp is a source dataset identifier).

provenance/input_inventory.json provides exact paths and checksums, including
the derived county admission series and saved model trajectories used as inputs
to later phases. Original acquisition timestamps and historical release dates
were not fully recorded. The imposed information lags are model assumptions;
the snapshots are not a collection of real-time data vintages.

Before public deposition, retain provider attribution and the provider-specific
terms. A deposit license should distinguish the software from third-party data.
