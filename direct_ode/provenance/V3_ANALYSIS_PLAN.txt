Post-review analysis plan for manuscript version 3
Recorded 17 October 2026 before calculating the new summaries

These analyses respond to the supplied AI pre-submission review. They are
post hoc additions. They do not change frozen phase protocols or their original
decisions, and are not an independent prospective validation experiment.

1. Re-score the five saved P0-P4 paths on wholly future calendar weeks.
Select target weeks whose start date is strictly later than the Saturday issue
date; index the first subsequent Sunday-starting week as future week 1. Compare
all three chains on common future weeks 1-7, summarizing weeks 1-4, 5-7 and 1-7.
Exclude P0_HINDSIGHT from the range. Omit missing observations, report counts and
individual future-week coverage, and describe P0 and persistence absolute errors.
Keep the original reporting-index results separately. Do not carry its frozen
50% continuation criterion forward as a newly prespecified prospective criterion;
show 50% only as the original descriptive reference.
Implementation note: inspect missing known admission weeks for additional shifts
in the original reporting grid. Retain available primary targets and report any
unavailable forecast; use the actual intersection of origin/calendar targets for
the matched comparison of chains, without filling an uncomputed prediction.

2. Re-score donor agreement under transparent alternative tolerances.
Use matched donor dates for L3 and L3B. Compare the original asymmetric band
[-0.10,+0.20] with symmetric bands +/-0.05, +/-0.10 and +/-0.20; require fractions
of 0.50, 2/3 and 0.80. Report all dates, 2020-2021 and 2022 separately, together
with unweighted absolute residual summaries. These are diagnostic sensitivity
checks, not interval estimates, survey-weighted analyses or assay corrections.
Do not change ascertainment calibration or refit the models.

3. Re-score the existing reconstruction summaries under alternative rules.
Cross total-admissions tolerances +/-10%, +/-20%, +/-30% with peak timing
tolerances of 7, 14 and 21 days. Keep the original serology rule for the joint
decision. Report the grid and original decisions without selecting a new winning
tolerance. This examines decision-rule sensitivity, not parameter sensitivity.

4. Clarify the implemented admission mapping and inspect saved fitted q values.
Derive the continuous-time E-to-I-to-outflow delay kernel for fixed sigma=1/5.1
and delta=1/7. Explain that q is constant within a fitted window and held at the
origin in projections, but varies between fitted windows and across stitched
weeks in retrospective reconstruction. Plot that saved variation and summarize
the existing lag diagnostics. No claim about the cause of drift will follow
from these descriptive checks.

5. Scope of unresolved requests.
No new severity/delay model, vaccine-parameter sweep, serokinetic model,
alternative behavioural fit, parameter bootstrap/posterior, formal identifiability
test, mechanistic competitor or untouched county is assumed to have been run.
The response record must distinguish completed checks from these open analyses.
All original sources, version 2 documents and frozen outputs remain unchanged.
