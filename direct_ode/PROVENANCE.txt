Release 1.2.0 addendum, 20 November 2026

Correction C1 was completed before this release: L2 and L3 were refitted with
the corrected ascertainment prior. The archived C1 fits are reused here;
the fresh replay does not repeat those fits. Scientific source files and all
32 expected paper tables and ten reference PNGs are unchanged from 1.1.0.

Recovered the three projection-replication scripts and their historical
outputs from the companion local development directory. Source paths and
hashes are in provenance/replication_source_inventory.json. These files are
preserved unchanged. Newly added analysis/verify_replication.py matches
calendar dates rather than assuming identical historical week indices.
The fresh replication and 48 tests support implementation consistency only;
the replication shares input modules, fitted states and the ODE integrator.

Verification files at the top of verification/ dated 18 October describe the
original 1.0.0 replay. The current C1 saved-fit replay is separately recorded
under verification/pre_submission_20261120/. The original eight unchanged
figures and the two C1 replacement figures are all preserved in reference_figures.
Original replication outputs remain under provenance/original_phase18b_replication.

The older unrecovered projection source remains unrecovered. No successful
replay or separate implementation is claimed to repair that historical gap.
